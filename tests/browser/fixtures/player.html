A stand-in for YouTube's embedded player, for tests/browser/capture.py. The check serves it at the player's own address, so its messages carry the player's origin. It speaks the part of the widget protocol the page uses: after "listening" it reports ready and the video's length and title; on "playVideo" it plays 440 Hz on the left and 1000 Hz on the right for the video's length, reports the time four times a second, and reports the end. The video id "blockedvid0" answers with error 150 instead, as a video kept off other sites does.
